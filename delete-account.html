Delete Your SplitHive Account

Last updated: 7 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SplitHive account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

You can permanently delete your account directly inside SplitHive at any time:

  1. Open the SplitHive app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and personal data are then permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we will delete it for you:

Email: nossoinfotech@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my account

We will verify your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account profile (name, email, and hashed password).
  • Trips you own, including their expenses, payments, loans, budgets, wallet balances, and categories.
  • Receipt images you uploaded.
  • Your personal/private expenses.

What data may be kept

  • Shared trips you did not create: If you were a member of a trip owned by someone else, the expenses and balances that other members rely on remain in that trip so their records stay accurate. Your personal profile is still removed, and your name may be shown as a non-account placeholder member.
  • Legal/operational records: We may retain limited records where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud. Such records are kept only as long as necessary and then deleted.

Retention period

Account and personal data deletion is effective immediately when you delete in the app, and within 30 days for email requests. Backup copies are purged on a rolling basis and fully removed within 90 days.

Contact

Questions about account deletion? Email nossoinfotech@gmail.com.

This page applies to the SplitHive mobile application for Android and iOS.
