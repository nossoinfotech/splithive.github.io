Privacy Policy for SplitHive Last updated: 7 June 2026 SplitHive (“we”, “our”, “the app”) helps groups split and track shared expenses for trips and events. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. 1. Information We Collect Account information: your name, email, and password. Passwords are never stored in plain text — only as a securely hashed value (bcrypt). Content you create: trips, group members, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories, payments, loans, budgets, wallet balances, and notes. Receipt images: photos you attach to expenses, stored securely so you and your trip members can view them. Device permissions: with your consent, the camera (to scan invite QR codes and photograph receipts) and photo library (to attach existing receipts). Requested only when you use the feature; you can decline or revoke anytime. Biometrics: fingerprint/Face ID (to protect private expenses) is handled entirely by your device OS — we never receive or store biometric data. 2. How We Use Your Information To create/secure your account; provide core features (splitting, balances, settlements, budgets, wallets, insights, reports); let you invite and join trips; convert currencies; and operate and improve the app. 3. Third-Party Services Exchange rates: daily reference rates from the public Frankfurter API — only currency codes are sent, no personal data. Cloud storage: receipts and app data are stored on secure managed cloud infrastructure. We do not sell your data and do not use advertising networks or ad tracking. 4. Information Sharing Trip content (expenses, amounts, member names, receipts) is visible to that trip's members. Private/personal expenses are excluded from shared totals and protected behind your device lock. We don't share personal data with other third parties except to operate the service or as required by law. 5. Data Retention & Deletion We keep data while your account is active. You can delete your account anytime in Profile → Delete account, which removes your personal account data. 6. Security Hashed passwords, authenticated API access, member-only trip access controls, and receipt file protections. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data. 7. Children's Privacy Not directed to children under 13 (or your country's minimum age); we don't knowingly collect their data. 8. Your Rights You may access, correct, or delete your personal data — most directly in the app, or by contacting us. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date. 10. Contact Us Email: nossoinfotech@gmail.com
