Frequently asked questions

How do I invite friends to a trip?

Open the trip and share its invite link or code. Friends tap "Join trip" on the Home screen and enter the code, or scan the invite QR code.

How are balances calculated?

Each expense is split between the chosen members. SplitHive adds up what each person paid and owes, converts currencies to the trip's main currency and shows the simplest way to settle up.

Why hasn't my payment changed the balance?

Settle-up payments only count once the person receiving the money confirms it. They'll see it under Balances and can confirm or decline.

How do I delete a payment?

A payment that hasn't been confirmed yet can be cancelled straight away. Once it's confirmed, both people must agree before it is deleted.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Add currencies in the trip's Settings. Live exchange rates convert everything to the trip's main currency.

Can I keep some expenses private?

Yes. Mark an expense as Private when adding it. Private expenses are excluded from shared totals and balances.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset code.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. Your account and personal data are removed. You can also email us to request deletion.
